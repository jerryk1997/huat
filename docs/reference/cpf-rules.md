## OW vs AW (the part generic apps get wrong)
- OW: monthly salary, capped per month at OW ceiling.
- AW: bonus/commission, capped at `annual ceiling − OW contributed YTD`.
- ⇒ a bonus's CPF depends on OW earlier that year. Huat logs bonuses as events and computes
  AW headroom at each.

## Milestones surfaced
- MA → BHS: overflow redirects (SA <55, RA ≥55); contribution behaviour changes.
- SA/RA → FRS/BRS: retirement adequacy; FRS headline.
- OA → $20k: only excess investable via CPFIS-OA.
- Top-up headroom: unused = forgone tax relief; prompt before year end.
- First $60k combined: +1% interest.

## Derived outputs
DOB + gross monthly salary → employee deduction, employer contribution, per-account split,
**take-home pay** (the budgeting number).
