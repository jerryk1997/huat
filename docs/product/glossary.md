## CPF
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CPF | Central Provident Fund — SG mandatory savings; employee + employer contribute % of salary |
| OA | Ordinary Account — housing, insurance, investment, education |
| SA | Special Account — retirement; higher interest, restricted |
| MA | MediSave Account — medical + approved health insurance |
| RA | Retirement Account — created at 55 from OA+SA; funds payouts |
| OW | Ordinary Wage — monthly salary; monthly ceiling |
| AW | Additional Wage — bonus/commission; annual ceiling |
| BHS | Basic Healthcare Sum — MA cap; excess → SA/RA |
| FRS | Full Retirement Sum — headline retirement target |
| BRS | Basic Retirement Sum — lower target, ~½ FRS |
| CPFIS | CPF Investment Scheme — invest OA/SA above thresholds |
| Top-up | Voluntary contribution (SA/RA or MA); tax relief |
