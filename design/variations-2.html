Round 2 — Three Fresh Directions

Each direction below shows light and dark mode side-by-side, focused on the Home screen (the daily-use surface). Same content, three different vibes.

Variation 5

Olive & Bone

Organic, considered, calm. Olive green and bone with a terracotta goal accent. Serif headlines on money for an editorial touch, ring-style progress indicators replace bars. Feels like Aesop's design language meets a hand-bound finance journal. Wellness-adjacent but grown-up.
