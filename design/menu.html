FAB Position — Floating vs Integrated
Floating + button (right-bottom): classic pattern, doesn't change tab bar layout. Adds visual weight in bottom-right. Easy to one-hand on iPhone. Recommended for expense tracking since quick-add is the most-used action and deserves the dominant position.

Integrated + button (centre of tab bar): elegant, removes a floating element. But it sits above the safe area and centre is awkward for right-handed thumb. Better when add-action is occasional, not constant.

My take: stick with floating right-bottom. The square shape + 1.5px border + 3px offset shadow ties it to the design system instead of looking like a sticker bolted on. Position above the tab bar with enough clearance so it doesn't cover content under it.
